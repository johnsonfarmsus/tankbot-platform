Mapping
Keys: arrows or W A S D to drive, Space stops, Esc cancels. Drag the map to look around, scroll or pinch to zoom, ◎ to follow the robot again.

Current map

Maps autosave every 20 s, and the last map loads when the brain starts. To edit walls or draw no-go lines, use Edit map on the Drive page.

Saved maps

Load switches to a saved map; the robot finds itself on it with the lidar. If it can't, drive a little, or put it on the home spot (white circle, facing along its line) and press I'm at home.

Robot

One place for the whole robot: for each sensor, what it is plugged into, what it is used for, which way it faces, and where it sits. Positions are from the platform's front and left edges (negative = sticking out ahead); heights are from the platform top (negative = below it). Saving stores the layout and sends sensor changes to the robot (it restarts once to apply them).
