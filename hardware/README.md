# TankBot 3D-printed parts

**Get the parts from Printables:** [TankBot on Printables](https://www.printables.com/model/1869360-tankbot).
That is the home of the TankBot parts: print settings, photos, updated parts and new additions all land
there first. Downloading, liking and posting your makes there also supports the project.

[`tankbot-modelfiles.zip`](tankbot-modelfiles.zip) here is a ready-to-go backup pack of the parts as
of this release, in case Printables is ever unavailable: `.stl` files to print and `.f3d` Fusion 360
sources. It is not updated as often as Printables.

**In the pack:** base plate, lidar tower and lidar base, phone base and phone clamp, bumper and bumper
switch mount, two-part ultrasonic mount, battery clamp, charger clamp (with and without a switch),
2-wire connector, wire management, and spacers (they sit between an electronics board and the base,
with the screw passing through both, to lift the board off).

**One screw for everything:** every printed part, board and module mounts with **M3 x 6 mm socket head
cap screws**.

The base robot (chassis, motors, original mounting parts) comes from the original
[TankBot ESP32](https://github.com/johnsonfarmsus/tank-bot-esp32) project and its
[Printables parts](https://www.printables.com/model/1516204-tank-bot-esp32).
